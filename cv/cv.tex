\vspace{-0.7cm}

I am an Owen Chamberlain Postdoctoral Fellow at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. I obtained my PhD in Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics from the University of Cambridge in July 2024 and a Bachelor of Science in Physics from Haverford College in May 2020. My interests lie at the intersection of theoretical and observational cosmology. I analyse the large-scale structure of the Universe using galaxy surveys and gravitational lensing of the cosmic microwave background. I develop and test new approaches and models to probe fundamental physics such as neutrino masses, dark matter properties, and structure formation.
