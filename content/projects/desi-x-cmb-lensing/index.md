I am leading the development and depolyment of the measurement and analysis pipeline for the cross-correlation of galaxies from the DESI Galaxy survey (Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument).

Together with my colleagues, I measure the correlation between the gravitaional lensing of the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) with the positions of millions of galaxies mapped by DESI.
