The ACT collaboration has released the DR6 maps, power spectra, likelihoods, and the corresponding cosmological constraints — a milestone the collaboration has been building toward for years. The main papers:

- [_DR6 power spectra, likelihoods and ΛCDM parameters_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14452) (Louis et al.)
- [_DR6 constraints on extended cosmological models_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14454) (Calabrese et al.)
- [_DR6 maps_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14451) (Naess et al.)
