Our paper [_The Atacama Cosmology Telescope: Multi-probe cosmology with unWISE galaxies and ACT DR6 CMB lensing_](/publications/farren-2024-rla/) is now on the arXiv ([2409.02109](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02109)) and submitted to Phys. Rev. D. It builds directly on our 2023 ApJ paper, jointly fitting galaxy auto-correlation, galaxy–lensing cross-correlation, and the CMB lensing auto-spectrum to deliver tighter constraints on structure growth.
